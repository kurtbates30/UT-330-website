9/28 Design Sprint

September 28, 2026 · Week 5

during this week my group focused on creating digital screens using Google's design system and Figma. The goal was to prototype a user interface for a reporting broken streetlights.

Design Summary

During our design sprint week I found it really easy to work and get along with my group. During phase 1 We focused on establishing the basic screens and overall flow before getting too deep into the design aspect. Having a good base to continue to build on will make it easier to tweak on during the other phases. Our challenge for phase 2 was to make it mandatory to add/take a photo of the lightpole. We implemented this into the first screen by adding some buttons at the bottom to attach or take a photo. For phase 3 we continued to iterate on our designs. Our change order was to add a new screen to select a new language. We took inspiration from Google and Apple's language screens.
